Finansipedia

Product DevelopmentFull-stack DevelopmentData EngineeringPythonFastAPINextJSPostgreSQLInfrastructure

Finansipedia is my biggest current project: an all-in-one research platform that makes Indonesian (IDX) stock analysis faster and more structured. Instead of opening several apps and spreadsheets to check one stock, investors get market data, screening and analysis on one screen. It is built around objective data rather than hype or trading signals.

 

The market section covers a dashboard with a market heatmap, market activity, sector performance, company announcements, commodities and a corporate-action calendar. Each stock page brings together charts, fundamentals and financial statements, broker flow, ownership history and a relation map of connected companies and shareholders.

 

For finding ideas, the app has a screener, special-notation and high-concentration screens, sector components, broker analysis and Tycoon Analysis, which tracks what controlling shareholders and large investors own. Users can keep watchlists, notes and a portfolio tracker, read AI summaries of company announcements, use calculators (average down, CAGR, target price, dividend, position sizing) and connect Telegram for alerts.

 

I build and run the whole stack myself: Python data pipelines that collect and process IDX market, financial and ownership data into a TimescaleDB/PostgreSQL database, a FastAPI backend with Redis caching, a Next.js web app, subscriptions and a paid public data API.
