PENNY TRACKER

PythonFlaskNextJSDockerWeb HostingData Analytics

PENNY TRACKER is designed to make personal finance management accessible, efficient, and insightful for everyone, from budgeting beginners to experienced investors. The platform offers a wide range of features to simplify financial tracking and analysis, empowering users to take full control of their financial lives.

 

With secure Google login, accessing your account is quick and hassle-free. Users can easily manage their financial data by exporting information to Excel with a single click or uploading transactions in bulk via Excel, saving time and effort. The platform also includes a robust stocks analysis tool, featuring candlestick charts for a detailed view of stock performance and daily automated stock recommendations, providing valuable insights to guide investment decisions.

 

Penny Tracker is continually evolving to deliver even more value. Future updates include AI-powered financial analysis, offering automated and actionable insights to optimize your financial strategies. The ability to upload e-statements will further streamline data entry, while data sharing features will enable collaboration and shared insights with trusted individuals. Additionally, the introduction of a Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline capabilities will ensure that you can manage your finances anytime, anywhere, even without an internet connection.

 

Currently in beta, Penny Tracker is actively improving with user feedback to fine-tune its features and enhance the overall experience. Whether you’re tracking transactions or diving deep into investment strategies, Penny Tracker is your go-to platform for smarter money management
